DOSSIER — FILE 001

From weapons dealer to world's shield

Every genius has an origin measured in loss. Stark's began in a cave, ended in a boardroom, and never really stopped.

The Ten Rings

During a weapons demonstration in Afghanistan, Stark is ambushed and gravely wounded by shrapnel bound for his heart. His captors demand he build them a missile. He builds an electromagnet-powered chest device and a suit of armor instead.

A Cave and a Torch

Working from salvaged parts and a car battery, Stark and fellow captive Ho Yinsen assemble the first arc reactor and the crude Mark I armor, escaping under his own power for the first time in his life.

Privatizing World Peace

Back home, Stark halts Stark Industries' weapons division overnight, redirects the company toward clean energy and defense technology, and quietly keeps building — this time by choice, not captivity.

"The device in my chest is the only thing keeping the shrapnel from killing me. It's also the reason I'm still useful." — attributed, Stark Industries internal memo
